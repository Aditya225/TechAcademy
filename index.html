About Us

We Train. We Place. We Build.

Tech Academy started as a mentoring platform. Today we are a complete tech solutions company serving three needs — individuals, companies, and businesses.

For Individuals: We train aspiring developers in Node.js, NestJS, PostgreSQL and help them land jobs at top companies.

For Companies: We source, screen and place pre-trained backend developers based on your exact requirements. Zero cost until you hire.

For Businesses: We build scalable backend systems, REST APIs, and AI-powered solutions for your product needs.
