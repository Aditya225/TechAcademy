What Techs Academy Does

From Understanding Skills to Building the Next Step.

Techs Academy is evolving beyond a technology training platform. We work with learners and professionals at different stages — helping them understand their current position, identify gaps and take practical next steps.

For Students: We help young learners explore interests, expectations, thinking patterns and career possibilities through structured guidance and development activities.

For Freshers & Job Seekers: We go beyond the resume with technical assessment, 1:1 mock interviews, project discussion and skill-gap feedback.

For Professionals: We help identify technical and interview-readiness gaps and build a focused improvement roadmap.

For Freshers & Job Seekers

Your Resume Is Only the Beginning.

For candidates, we look beyond keywords. The goal is to understand what you know, what you can explain, and where you need to improve.

1. Resume Review
We review your resume against your target role and identify claims, gaps and areas that need stronger evidence.

2. Technical Assessment
We assess fundamentals, practical understanding and problem-solving through role-relevant questions or tasks.

3. 1:1 Mock Interview
We observe how you explain projects, approach technical questions and communicate under interview pressure.

4. Skill-Gap Review
You receive structured feedback on technical skills, communication and interview readiness.

5. Improvement Roadmap
We identify what to work on next so your preparation becomes focused instead of random.

Meet The Founder
Aditya Narayan Gaur
Founder · Technical Mentor · Career & Student Development

Hi! I’m Aditya, the founder of Techs Academy.

My journey in technology has given me the opportunity to work as a developer, collaborate with technical teams, understand real-world engineering challenges, and work with people at different stages of their learning and career journeys.

Over time, I noticed a common gap.

Many students complete courses, collect certificates, build projects, and add skills to their resumes — yet when they face a technical discussion, assessment, or interview, they often struggle to explain what they have learned, solve problems independently, or understand what they actually need to improve.

At the same time, many job seekers know they need to improve their skills, resume, or interview preparation, but don't always know where the actual gap is or what they should focus on next.

Why I Started Techs Academy

Techs Academy was created to address this gap.

Our goal is not simply to teach another technology or help someone complete another course.

We want to help learners understand where they are today, identify their gaps, and build a practical path toward where they want to go.

Our Approach

I believe good mentorship is not about giving everyone the same roadmap.

It is about understanding the individual, identifying what needs improvement, and helping them take the right next step.

Every learner has a different starting point, different strengths, different challenges, and different goals.

Understand → Assess → Identify Gaps → Guide → Practice → Improve

The objective is not to make students dependent on a mentor.

It is to help them gradually develop the ability to think, learn, solve problems, communicate their knowledge, and make better career decisions independently.
