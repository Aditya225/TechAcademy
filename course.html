Frequently Asked Questions

Are these courses suitable for freshers?

Yes. The learning path can start from fundamentals and gradually move toward projects and advanced concepts depending on your current level.

Do I need prior programming experience?

It depends on the program. Some programs can start from fundamentals, while advanced backend, AI or cloud topics are better suited to learners who already understand programming basics.

Will I work on practical projects?

Yes. Practical implementation is an important part of the learning approach. Projects can be selected according to the learner's level and program.

Do you provide career or resume guidance?

Yes. Techs Academy also provides career guidance, resume optimization and interview-oriented support as separate services.

Do you provide certificates?

Course completion documentation or certificates can be provided according to the program and applicable business requirements. A course certificate should not be represented as a government or university qualification.

Can companies request customized training?

Yes. Companies can contact Techs Academy for customized technical workshops and training requirements.
